This directory contains non-normative laboratory exercises for the Chemical sector under the CAI USA framework. These labs do not assert compliance, certification, accreditation, or regulatory approval.

Purpose

The purpose of the Chemical sector labs is to demonstrate controlled, auditable, and safety-aware application of CAI concepts within chemical production, processing, storage, and distribution environments. These labs provide practical illustrations of how CAI telemetry normalization, evidence handling, correlation, and human-governed decision support may be exercised without modifying CAI CORE semantics, CAI USA baseline governance, or the frozen Chemical sector overlay.

Non-Normative Nature

All laboratories contained in this directory are non-normative. They do not introduce requirements, controls, or authoritative guidance. They exist solely to illustrate operational patterns and analytical workflows aligned with CAI principles. No laboratory content overrides sector definitions, safety constraints, AI governance rules, or operational authority.

Relationship to Frozen Chemical Sector Overlay

These laboratories operate strictly in support of the frozen CAI USA Chemical sector overlay. They do not redefine scope, assumptions, threat models, secure-by-design controls, AI governance constraints, telemetry definitions, or operational playbooks established under the Chemical sector documents. Any discrepancy between labs and authoritative sector artifacts must be resolved in favor of the sector overlay.

Laboratory Scope

Chemical sector laboratories focus on the following illustrative areas:
Telemetry normalization and evidence preservation in chemical environments
State-aware correlation across IT, OT, and process-adjacent systems
Human-governed analytical decision support under safety-dominant constraints
Controlled observation of anomalies without autonomous enforcement

Laboratories explicitly avoid autonomous control, process manipulation, or safety-critical execution.

Artificial Intelligence Governance in Labs

Artificial Intelligence within Chemical sector labs is strictly governed by CAI CORE and CAI USA AI governance rules.
