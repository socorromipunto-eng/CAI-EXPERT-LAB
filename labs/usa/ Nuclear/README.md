# CAI USA — Nuclear Labs (Non-Normative)

## Overview

This directory contains **non-normative laboratory artifacts** for the **CAI USA Nuclear
sector**. These laboratories demonstrate how CAI concepts are applied within nuclear
environments in a **highly conservative, safety-first, and strictly human-governed** manner.

The Nuclear labs exist to validate **design intent**, **governance boundaries**, **evidence
handling**, and **explicit AI non-autonomy** without modifying CAI CORE, the CAI USA baseline,
or asserting regulatory, safety, or legal compliance.

---

## Non-Normative Scope

All artifacts contained in this directory are **laboratory demonstrations only**.

They:
- Do **not** define nuclear safety analyses or reactor assessments
- Do **not** authorize interaction with safety or reactor protection systems
- Do **not** assert compliance with NRC, DOE, IAEA, or other nuclear authorities
- Do **not** authorize operational, safety, or configuration changes
- Do **not** replace nuclear safety governance or regulatory oversight

Normative CAI documentation remains authoritative at all times.

---

## Purpose of Nuclear Labs

The Nuclear laboratories are designed to demonstrate:

- CCES-compliant telemetry normalization in nuclear-support environments
- State-aware correlation under conservative operational assumptions
- Governed telemetry flow and evidence generation
- **Strictly advisory, human-governed decision support**
- Explicit **AI governance and non-autonomy**

These labs emphasize that **nuclear safety, conservatism, and human authority dominate all
analysis and decision-support activities**.

---

## Role of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is present in all Nuclear labs as a **governed analytical support
component**.

Across all Nuclear laboratories:
- AI is **strictly advisory**
- AI does **not** assess reactor conditions or safety margins
- AI does **not** advance lifecycle states
- AI does **not** trigger operational or safety actions
- AI does **not** override human authority
- AI behavior is **bounded, explainable, and auditable**

AI exists to support human understanding in safety-critical nuclear contexts, not to automate
decisions.

## Nuclear Laboratory Artifacts

The Nuclear sector includes the following laboratory artifacts:

- **CCES Normalization**  
  `CAI-USA-Nuclear-Lab-CCES-Normalization.md`

  `CAI-USA-Nuclear-Lab-State-Aware-Correlation.md`

- **Telemetry Flow and Evidence**  
  `CAI-USA-Nuclear-Lab-Telemetry-Flow-and-Evidence.md`  
  Demonstrates governed end-to-end telemetry flow and evidence generation, ensuring chain-of-
  custody, integrity, and audit readiness in nuclear environments.

- **Human-Governed Decision Support**  
  `CAI-USA-Nuclear-Lab-Human-Governed-Decision-Support.md`  
  Demonstrates strictly advisory, explainable decision support with mandatory human authority
  and explicit non-autonomy.
