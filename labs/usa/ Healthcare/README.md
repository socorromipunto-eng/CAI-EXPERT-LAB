# CAI USA — Healthcare Labs (Non-Normative)

## Overview

This directory contains **non-normative laboratory artifacts** for the **CAI USA Healthcare
sector**. These laboratories demonstrate how CAI concepts are applied within healthcare
environments in a **controlled, safety-aware, and human-governed** manner.

The Healthcare labs exist to validate **design intent**, **governance boundaries**,
**evidentiary handling**, and **mandatory human authority** without modifying CAI CORE,
the CAI USA baseline, or asserting regulatory, clinical, or legal compliance.

---

## Non-Normative Scope

All artifacts contained in this directory are **laboratory demonstrations only**.

They:
- Do **not** define clinical workflows, diagnoses, or treatment decisions
- Do **not** authorize interaction with live medical devices or patient-care systems
- Do **not** assert compliance with HIPAA, HITECH, FDA, or other healthcare mandates
- Do **not** replace clinical governance, patient safety controls, or legal oversight

Normative CAI documentation remains authoritative at all times.

---

## Purpose of Healthcare Labs

The Healthcare laboratories are designed to demonstrate:

- CCES-compliant telemetry normalization in clinical-support environments
- State-aware correlation preserving patient safety and care continuity
- Governed telemetry flow and evidence generation
- **Strictly advisory, human-governed decision support**
- Explicit **AI governance and non-autonomy**

These labs emphasize that **patient safety, clinical continuity, and human authority
dominate all analysis and decision-support activities**.

---

## Role of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is present in all Healthcare labs as a **governed analytical support
component**.

Across all Healthcare laboratories:
- AI is **strictly advisory**
- AI does **not** diagnose, treat, or infer clinical outcomes
- AI does **not** advance lifecycle states
- AI does **not** trigger clinical, operational, or security actions
- AI behavior is **bounded, explainable, and auditable**

AI exists to support human understanding in safety-critical healthcare contexts, not to
automate patient-care decisions.

## Healthcare Laboratory Artifacts

The Healthcare sector includes the following laboratory artifacts:

- **CCES Normalization**  
  `CAI-USA-Healthcare-Lab-CCES-Normalization.md`  
  Demonstrates deterministic normalization of Healthcare telemetry into CCES while preserving
  patient safety context, clinical boundaries, and evidentiary traceability.

- **State-Aware Correlation**  
  `CAI-USA-Healthcare-Lab-State-Aware-Correlation.md`  
  Demonstrates correlation of CCES-normalized telemetry with explicit awareness of clinical
  system state, service continuity, and safety constraints.

- **Telemetry Flow and Evidence**  
  `CAI-USA-Healthcare-Lab-Telemetry-Flow-and-Evidence.md`  
  Demonstrates governed end-to-end telemetry flow and evidence generation, ensuring chain-of-
  custody, integrity, and audit readiness in healthcare environments.

- **Human-Governed Decision Support**  
  `CAI-USA-Healthcare-Lab-Human-Governed-Decision-Support.md`  
  Demonstrates strictly advisory, explainable decision support with mandatory human authority
  and explicit non-autonomy.
