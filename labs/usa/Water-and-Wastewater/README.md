# CAI USA — Water and Wastewater Labs (Non-Normative)

## Overview

This directory contains **non-normative laboratory artifacts** for the **CAI USA Water and
Wastewater sector**. These laboratories demonstrate how CAI concepts are applied within
public-health–critical environments under **conservative operational assumptions**, **explicit
AI governance**, and **mandatory human authority**.

The Water and Wastewater labs exist to validate **design intent**, **governance boundaries**,
**evidence handling**, and **non-autonomous AI behavior** without modifying CAI CORE, the
CAI USA baseline, or asserting regulatory, safety, or operational compliance.

---

## Non-Normative Scope

All artifacts contained in this directory are **laboratory demonstrations only**.

They:
- Do **not** authorize interaction with live treatment, distribution, or control systems
- Do **not** infer water quality, treatment efficacy, or regulatory compliance
- Do **not** authorize chemical dosing, flow control, or process changes
- Do **not** assert compliance with EPA, DHS, CISA, NIST, or state water authorities
- Do **not** replace public health governance or utility oversight

Normative CAI documentation remains authoritative at all times.

---

## Purpose of Water and Wastewater Labs

The Water and Wastewater laboratories are designed to demonstrate:

- Deterministic **CCES-compliant telemetry normalization**
- **State-aware correlation** under public health and service continuity constraints
- Governed **telemetry flow and evidence generation**
- **Strictly advisory, human-governed decision support**
- Explicit **AI non-autonomy** in public-health–sensitive environments

These labs emphasize that **public health protection, operational conservatism, and human
authority dominate all analysis and decision-support activities**.

---

## Role of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is present in all Water and Wastewater labs as a **governed analytical
support component**.

Across all Water and Wastewater laboratories:
- AI is **strictly advisory**
- AI does **not** interpret water quality or safety margins
- AI does **not** infer regulatory compliance
- AI does **not** advance lifecycle states
- AI does **not** trigger operational or chemical actions
- AI does **not** override human authority
- AI behavior is **bounded, explainable, and auditable**

AI exists to support conservative human understanding, not to automate public health decisions.

## Water and Wastewater Laboratory Artifacts

The Water and Wastewater sector includes the following laboratory artifacts:

- **CCES Normalization**  
  `CAI-USA-Water-and-Wastewater-Lab-CCES-Normalization.md`  
  Demonstrates deterministic normalization of heterogeneous Water and Wastewater telemetry
  into CCES while preserving public health sensitivity, operational context, and evidentiary
  traceability.

- **State-Aware Correlation**  
  `CAI-USA-Water-and-Wastewater-Lab-State-Aware-Correlation.md`  
  Demonstrates governed correlation of CCES-normalized telemetry with explicit awareness of
  system state, service continuity, and public health constraints.

- **Telemetry Flow and Evidence**  
  `CAI-USA-Water-and-Wastewater-Lab-Telemetry-Flow-and-Evidence.md`  
  Demonstrates controlled end-to-end telemetry flow and evidence generation ensuring chain-of-
  custody, integrity, and audit readiness.

- **Human-Governed Decision Support**  
  `CAI-USA-Water-and-Wastewater-Lab-Human-Governed-Decision-Support.md`  
  Demonstrates strictly advisory, explainable decision support with mandatory human authority
  and explicit non-autonomy.
