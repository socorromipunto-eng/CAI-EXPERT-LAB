# CAI USA — Emergency Services Labs (Non-Normative)

## Overview

This directory contains **non-normative laboratory artifacts** for the **CAI USA Emergency
Services sector**. These labs demonstrate how CAI concepts are applied within emergency
response, public safety, and life-critical service environments in a **controlled,
experimental, and non-authoritative** manner.

The Emergency Services labs exist to validate **design intent**, **governance boundaries**,
**evidence handling**, and **mandatory human authority** without modifying CAI CORE,
the CAI USA baseline, or asserting regulatory compliance.

---

## Non-Normative Scope

All artifacts contained in this directory are **laboratory demonstrations only**.

They:
- Do **not** define emergency response procedures or operational requirements
- Do **not** authorize dispatch, field actions, or life-safety decisions
- Do **not** assert compliance, certification, or regulatory approval
- Do **not** replace emergency management, public safety, or legal governance

Normative CAI documentation remains authoritative at all times.

---

## Purpose of Emergency Services Labs

The Emergency Services laboratories are designed to demonstrate:

- CCES-compliant telemetry normalization for emergency environments
- State-aware correlation under life-safety and urgency constraints
- Governed telemetry flow and evidence generation
- **Strictly advisory, human-governed decision support**
- Explicit **AI governance and non-autonomy**

These labs emphasize that **life safety, human authority, and operational continuity
dominate all analysis and decision-support activities**.

---

## Role of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is present in all Emergency Services labs as a **governed analytical
support component**.

Across all Emergency Services laboratories:
- AI is **strictly advisory**
- AI does **not** execute actions or trigger dispatch
- AI does **not** advance lifecycle states
- AI does **not** override human authority
- AI behavior is **bounded, explainable, and auditable**

AI exists to support human understanding in high-pressure environments, not to automate
life-critical decisions.

## Emergency Services Laboratory Artifacts

The Emergency Services sector includes the following laboratory artifacts:

- **CCES Normalization**  
  `CAI-USA-Emergency-Services-Lab-CCES-Normalization.md`  
  Demonstrates deterministic normalization of Emergency Services telemetry into CCES while
  preserving life-safety context, urgency indicators, and evidentiary traceability.

- **State-Aware Correlation**  
  `CAI-USA-Emergency-Services-Lab-State-Aware-Correlation.md`  
  Demonstrates correlation of CCES-normalized telemetry with explicit awareness of incident
  state, escalation level, and service continuity.

- **Telemetry Flow and Evidence**  
  `CAI-USA-Emergency-Services-Lab-Telemetry-Flow-and-Evidence.md`  
  Demonstrates governed end-to-end telemetry flow and evidence generation, ensuring chain-of-
  custody, integrity, and audit readiness in emergency environments.

- **Human-Governed Decision Support**  
  `CAI-USA-Emergency-Services-Lab-Human-Governed-Decision-Support.md`  
  Demonstrates strictly advisory, explainable decision support with mandatory human authority
  and explicit non-autonomy.
