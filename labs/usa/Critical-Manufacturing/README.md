# CAI USA — Critical Manufacturing Labs (Non-Normative)

## Overview

This directory contains **non-normative laboratory artifacts** for the **CAI USA Critical
Manufacturing sector**. These labs demonstrate how CAI concepts are applied within
critical manufacturing environments in a **controlled, experimental, and non-authoritative**
manner.

The Critical Manufacturing labs exist to validate **design intent**, **governance boundaries**,
**evidence handling**, and **human authority enforcement** without modifying CAI CORE,
the CAI USA baseline, or asserting regulatory compliance.

---

## Non-Normative Scope

All artifacts contained in this directory are **laboratory demonstrations only**.

They:
- Do **not** define operational or regulatory requirements
- Do **not** authorize production, manufacturing, or control actions
- Do **not** assert compliance, certification, or regulatory approval
- Do **not** replace safety, quality, or industrial governance obligations

Normative CAI documents remain authoritative at all times.

---

## Purpose of Critical Manufacturing Labs

The Critical Manufacturing laboratories are designed to demonstrate:

- CCES-compliant telemetry normalization for industrial environments
- State-aware correlation under process safety and production continuity constraints
- Governed telemetry flow and evidence generation
- **Strictly advisory, human-governed decision support**
- Explicit **AI governance and non-autonomy**

These labs emphasize that **process safety, product quality, and production continuity
dominate all analysis and decision-support activities**.

---

## Role of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is present in all Critical Manufacturing labs as a **governed
analytical support component**.

Across all Critical Manufacturing laboratories:
- AI is **strictly advisory**
- AI does **not** execute manufacturing or control actions
- AI does **not** advance lifecycle states
- AI does **not** override human authority
- AI behavior is **bounded, explainable, and auditable**

AI exists to support human understanding, not to automate manufacturing decisions.

## Critical Manufacturing Laboratory Artifacts

The Critical Manufacturing sector includes the following laboratory artifacts:

- **CCES Normalization**  
  `CAI-USA-Critical-Manufacturing-Lab-CCES-Normalization.md`  
  Demonstrates deterministic normalization of industrial telemetry into CCES while preserving
  process safety context, production continuity, and evidentiary traceability.

- **State-Aware Correlation**  
  `CAI-USA-Critical-Manufacturing-Lab-State-Aware-Correlation.md`  
  Demonstrates correlation of CCES-normalized telemetry with explicit awareness of operational
  state, process safety conditions, and production criticality.

- **Telemetry Flow and Evidence**  
  `CAI-USA-Critical-Manufacturing-Lab-Telemetry-Flow-and-Evidence.md`  
  Demonstrates governed end-to-end telemetry flow and evidence generation, ensuring chain-of-
  custody, integrity, and audit readiness.

- **Human-Governed Decision Support**  
  `CAI-USA-Critical-Manufacturing-Lab-Human-Governed-Decision-Support.md`  
  Demonstrates strictly advisory, explainable decision support with mandatory human authority
  and explicit non-autonomy.
