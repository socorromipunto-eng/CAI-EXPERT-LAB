NOTE
This document is non-normative. It demonstrates laboratory behavior for state-aware
correlation within Critical Manufacturing environments and does not define authoritative
execution requirements.

Purpose
The purpose of this laboratory is to demonstrate how CAI performs state-aware correlation
of CCES-normalized Critical Manufacturing telemetry while preserving process safety,
production continuity, evidentiary traceability, and explicit AI governance.

This lab validates that correlation outputs are strictly analytical artifacts and never
executable, authoritative, or autonomous.

Scope
This laboratory applies to USA Critical Manufacturing non-production environments,
including discrete and continuous manufacturing operations supported by IT, OT, and
ICS infrastructures.

This lab does not:
• Execute manufacturing or production actions
• Interact with safety systems or control logic
• Trigger containment, isolation, or remediation
• Advance operational or lifecycle states

The lab focuses exclusively on governed analytical correlation.

State-Aware Correlation in CAI
State-aware correlation evaluates CCES-normalized telemetry in the context of:
• Operational and production state
• Process safety conditions
• Asset, system, and production criticality
• Temporal sequencing and dependency

Correlation explicitly avoids conclusions detached from operational or safety context.

AI Role and Governance in State-Aware Correlation
Artificial Intelligence is present as a governed analytical support component operating
under CAI Core integrity and governance constraints.

AI capabilities are limited to:
• Assisting correlation pattern recognition
• Supporting confidence scoring and uncertainty estimation
• Highlighting contextual inconsistencies or anomalous sequences
• Providing explainable correlation rationale

AI does not:
• Trigger actions or enforcement
• Recommend remediation or process intervention
• Advance lifecycle states
• Override correlation rules or safety constraints
• Operate outside CAI governance boundaries

All AI involvement is bounded, explainable, auditable, and strictly non-autonomous.

Correlation Inputs
The lab consumes:
• CCES-normalized telemetry
• Production and operational state indicators
• Asset, system, and process context
• Temporal ordering and dependency metadata
• Safety and quality constraints

No single signal is sufficient to produce correlated intelligence.

Correlation Outputs
The lab produces structured correlation artifacts including:
• Correlated event groupings
• Contextual state and production annotations
• Confidence and uncertainty indicators
• Explicit correlation rationale
• Explicit non-recommended interpretations

Outputs are analytical and descriptive, not prescriptive.

Human Authority Enforcement
All correlation outputs require human interpretation and validation.

CAI does not:
• Trigger enforcement
• Advance operational states
• Approve actions
• Override human authority

Human decision-makers retain full accountability.

Evidence and Auditability
Correlation artifacts generate immutable evidence including:
• Input references and provenance
• Correlation logic and rationale
• AI-assisted analytical indicators
• Temporal and state context
• Human acknowledgment requirement

These artifacts support audit, investigation, and forensic reconstruction.

Critical Manufacturing–Specific Constraints
In Critical Manufacturing environments:
• Process safety overrides analytic urgency
• Production continuity dominates correlation framing
• Ambiguity defaults to observation
• Human review is mandatory prior to escalation

Correlation must never pressure operators toward action.
