# CAI USA — Energy Labs (Non-Normative)

## Overview

This directory contains **non-normative laboratory artifacts** for the **CAI USA Energy sector**.
These labs demonstrate how CAI concepts are applied to Energy environments in a **controlled,
experimental, and non-authoritative** manner.

The Energy labs exist to validate **design intent**, **governance boundaries**, and **evidence
handling behavior** without modifying CAI CORE, the CAI USA baseline, or asserting regulatory
compliance.

---

## Non-Normative Scope

All artifacts contained in this directory are **laboratory demonstrations only**.

They:
- Do **not** define operational requirements
- Do **not** authorize execution or enforcement
- Do **not** assert compliance, certification, or regulatory approval
- Do **not** replace sector safety, operational, or legal governance

Normative CAI documents remain authoritative at all times.

---

## Purpose of Energy Labs

The Energy sector laboratories are designed to demonstrate:

- CCES-compliant telemetry normalization for Energy IT, OT, and ICS environments
- State-aware correlation under operational and safety constraints
- Governed telemetry flow and evidence generation
- **Strictly advisory, human-governed decision support**
- Explicit **AI governance and non-autonomy**

These labs emphasize that **safety, grid stability, and human authority dominate all analysis**.

---

## Role of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is present in all Energy labs as a **governed analytical support
component**.

Across all Energy laboratories:
- AI is **strictly advisory**
- AI does **not** execute actions
- AI does **not** advance lifecycle states
- AI does **not** override human authority
- AI behavior is **bounded, explainable, and auditable**

AI exists to support human understanding, not to automate control.

## Energy Laboratory Artifacts

The Energy sector includes the following laboratory artifacts:

- **CCES Normalization**  
  `CAI-USA-Energy-Lab-CCES-Normalization.md`  
  Demonstrates deterministic normalization of Energy-sector telemetry into CCES while preserving
  safety context, provenance, and evidentiary integrity.

- **State-Aware Correlation**  
  `CAI-USA-Energy-Lab-State-Aware-Correlation.md`  
  Demonstrates correlation of CCES-normalized telemetry with explicit awareness of operational
  state, asset criticality, and safety constraints.

- **Telemetry Flow and Evidence**  
  `CAI-USA-Energy-Lab-Telemetry-Flow-and-Evidence.md`  
  Demonstrates governed end-to-end telemetry flow and evidence generation, ensuring chain-of-
  custody, integrity, and audit readiness.

- **Human-Governed Decision Support**  
  `CAI-USA-Energy-Lab-Human-Governed-Decision-Support.md`  
  Demonstrates strictly advisory, explainable decision support with mandatory human authority and
  explicit non-autonomy.
