NOTE
This document is non-normative. It demonstrates laboratory behavior for state-aware correlation
within Energy sector environments and does not define authoritative execution requirements.

Purpose
The purpose of this laboratory is to demonstrate how CAI performs state-aware correlation of
CCES-normalized Energy-sector telemetry while preserving operational safety, system stability,
evidentiary traceability, and explicit AI governance.

This lab validates that correlated intelligence is produced strictly as an analytical artifact
and never as an executable or authoritative action.

Scope
This laboratory applies to USA Energy non-production environments, including generation,
transmission, distribution, and supporting ICS / OT systems.

This lab does not:
• Execute control, protection, or switching actions
• Trigger containment, isolation, or remediation
• Advance operational or lifecycle states
• Interact with safety interlocks or protection mechanisms

The lab focuses exclusively on correlation logic and governed analytical interpretation.

State-Aware Correlation in CAI
State-aware correlation in CAI evaluates CCES-normalized telemetry in the context of:
• Operational state
• Asset criticality
• Temporal sequencing
• Safety and protection boundaries

Correlation explicitly avoids generating conclusions detached from operational context.

AI Role and Governance in State-Aware Correlation
In this laboratory, Artificial Intelligence is present as a governed analytical support component
operating under CAI Core integrity and governance constraints.

AI capabilities are limited to:
• Assisting correlation pattern recognition
• Supporting confidence scoring and uncertainty estimation
• Highlighting contextual inconsistencies or anomalous sequences
• Providing explainable correlation rationale

AI does not:
• Trigger actions or responses
• Recommend remediation or enforcement
• Advance lifecycle states
• Override correlation rules or safety constraints
• Operate outside CAI governance boundaries

All AI involvement is bounded, explainable, auditable, and strictly non-autonomous.

Correlation Inputs
The lab consumes:
• CCES-normalized telemetry
• Operational state indicators
• Asset and system criticality context
• Temporal ordering and sequencing metadata
• Safety and protection constraints

No single signal is sufficient to produce correlated intelligence.

Correlation Outputs
The lab produces structured correlation artifacts including:
• Correlated event groupings
• Contextual state annotations
• Confidence and uncertainty indicators
• Explicit correlation rationale
• Non-recommended interpretations

Outputs are analytical and descriptive, not prescriptive.

Human Authority Enforcement
All correlated outputs are subject to human interpretation.
CAI does not:
• Trigger enforcement
• Advance operational states
• Approve actions
• Override operator authority

Human decision-makers retain full accountability.

Evidence and Auditability
Correlation artifacts generate immutable evidence including:
• Input references
• Correlation logic and rationale
• AI-assisted analytical indicators
• Temporal and state context
• Human acknowledgment requirement

These artifacts support audit, investigation, and forensic reconstruction.

Energy-Specific Constraints
In Energy environments:
• Grid stability and safety override analytic urgency
• Ambiguity defaults to observation
• Correlation must not pressure operators toward action
• Human review is mandatory prior to escalation

Correlation must never compromise safety or availability.
