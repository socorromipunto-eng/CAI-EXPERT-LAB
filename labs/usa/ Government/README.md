# CAI USA — Government Labs (Non-Normative)

## Overview

This directory contains **non-normative laboratory artifacts** for the **CAI USA Government
sector**. These labs demonstrate how CAI concepts are applied within Government
environments in a **controlled, transparent, and human-governed** manner.

The Government labs exist to validate **design intent**, **governance boundaries**,
**evidentiary handling**, and **mandatory human authority** without modifying CAI CORE,
the CAI USA baseline, or asserting regulatory, policy, or legal compliance.

---

## Non-Normative Scope

All artifacts contained in this directory are **laboratory demonstrations only**.

They:
- Do **not** define policy, legal, or regulatory requirements
- Do **not** authorize administrative or enforcement actions
- Do **not** assert compliance with FISMA, FedRAMP, NIST, or other mandates
- Do **not** replace democratic, institutional, or legal governance

Normative CAI documentation remains authoritative at all times.

---

## Purpose of Government Labs

The Government laboratories are designed to demonstrate:

- CCES-compliant telemetry normalization under institutional constraints
- State-aware correlation preserving public accountability and oversight
- Governed telemetry flow and evidence generation
- **Strictly advisory, human-governed decision support**
- Explicit **AI governance and non-autonomy**

These labs emphasize that **democratic accountability, transparency, and human authority
dominate all analysis and decision-support activities**.

---

## Role of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is present in all Government labs as a **governed analytical
support component**.

Across all Government laboratories:
- AI is **strictly advisory**
- AI does **not** execute actions or interpret policy
- AI does **not** advance lifecycle states
- AI does **not** override institutional or democratic authority
- AI behavior is **bounded, explainable, and auditable**

AI exists to support lawful human decision-making, not to automate governance.

## Government Laboratory Artifacts

The Government sector includes the following laboratory artifacts:

- **CCES Normalization**  
  `CAI-USA-Government-Lab-CCES-Normalization.md`  
  Demonstrates deterministic normalization of Government telemetry into CCES while
  preserving institutional integrity, public accountability, and evidentiary traceability.

- **State-Aware Correlation**  
  `CAI-USA-Government-Lab-State-Aware-Correlation.md`  
  Demonstrates correlation of CCES-normalized telemetry with explicit awareness of
  institutional state, service continuity, and oversight boundaries.

- **Telemetry Flow and Evidence**  
  `CAI-USA-Government-Lab-Telemetry-Flow-and-Evidence.md`  
  Demonstrates governed end-to-end telemetry flow and evidence generation, ensuring chain-of-
  custody, integrity, and audit readiness.

- **Human-Governed Decision Support**  
  `CAI-USA-Government-Lab-Human-Governed-Decision-Support.md`  
  Demonstrates strictly advisory, explainable decision support with mandatory human authority
  and explicit non-autonomy.
