# CAI USA — Defense Industrial Base Labs (Non-Normative)

## Overview

This directory contains **non-normative laboratory artifacts** for the **CAI USA Defense
Industrial Base (DIB) sector**. These labs demonstrate how CAI concepts are applied within
Defense Industrial Base environments in a **controlled, sensitivity-aware, and
non-authoritative** manner.

The Defense Industrial Base labs exist to validate **design intent**, **governance boundaries**,
**evidence handling**, **supply chain trust**, and **mandatory human authority** without
modifying CAI CORE, the CAI USA baseline, or asserting regulatory compliance.

---

## Non-Normative Scope

All artifacts contained in this directory are **laboratory demonstrations only**.

They:
- Do **not** define operational, contractual, or regulatory requirements
- Do **not** authorize mission execution, production, or engineering actions
- Do **not** assert compliance, certification, or accreditation
- Do **not** replace national security, contractual, or supply chain governance obligations

Normative CAI documentation remains authoritative at all times.

---

## Purpose of Defense Industrial Base Labs

The Defense Industrial Base laboratories are designed to demonstrate:

- CCES-compliant telemetry normalization under national security sensitivity
- State-aware correlation with trust-domain and program context
- Governed telemetry flow and evidence generation
- **Strictly advisory, human-governed decision support**
- Explicit **AI governance and non-autonomy**

These labs emphasize that **national security sensitivity, supply chain trust, and human
authority dominate all analysis and decision-support activities**.

---

## Role of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is present in all Defense Industrial Base labs as a **governed
analytical support component**.

Across all DIB laboratories:
- AI is **strictly advisory**
- AI does **not** execute actions or infer mission intent
- AI does **not** advance lifecycle states
- AI does **not** override human or program authority
- AI behavior is **bounded, explainable, and auditable**

AI exists to support cleared human judgment, not to automate defense decisions.

  `CAI-USA-Defense-Industrial-Base-Lab-Human-Governed-Decision-Support.md`  
  Demonstrates strictly advisory, explainable decision support with mandatory human authority
