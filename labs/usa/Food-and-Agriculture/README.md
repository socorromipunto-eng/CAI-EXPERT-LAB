# CAI USA — Food and Agriculture Labs (Non-Normative)

## Overview

This directory contains **non-normative laboratory artifacts** for the **CAI USA Food and
Agriculture sector**. These labs demonstrate how CAI concepts are applied within food
production, processing, storage, and supply chain environments in a **controlled,
experimental, and non-authoritative** manner.

The Food and Agriculture labs exist to validate **design intent**, **governance boundaries**,
**evidence handling**, and **human authority enforcement** without modifying CAI CORE,
the CAI USA baseline, or asserting regulatory compliance.

---

## Non-Normative Scope

All artifacts contained in this directory are **laboratory demonstrations only**.

They:
- Do **not** define operational or regulatory requirements
- Do **not** authorize execution, production changes, or enforcement
- Do **not** assert compliance, certification, or regulatory approval
- Do **not** replace food safety, quality assurance, or supply chain governance

Normative CAI documents remain authoritative at all times.

---

## Purpose of Food and Agriculture Labs

The Food and Agriculture laboratories are designed to demonstrate:

- CCES-compliant telemetry normalization for Food and Agriculture environments
- State-aware correlation under food safety, quality, and supply chain constraints
- Governed telemetry flow and evidence generation
- **Strictly advisory, human-governed decision support**
- Explicit **AI governance and non-autonomy**

These labs emphasize that **food safety, public health, and supply chain integrity
dominate all analysis and decision-support activities**.

---

## Role of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is present in all Food and Agriculture labs as a **governed
analytical support component**.

Across all Food and Agriculture laboratories:
- AI is **strictly advisory**
- AI does **not** execute actions or alter production
- AI does **not** advance lifecycle states
- AI does **not** override human authority
- AI behavior is **bounded, explainable, and auditable**

AI exists to support human understanding, not to automate food safety or production decisions.

## Food and Agriculture Laboratory Artifacts

The Food and Agriculture sector includes the following laboratory artifacts:

- **CCES Normalization**  
  `CAI-USA-Food-and-Agriculture-Lab-CCES-Normalization.md`  
  Demonstrates deterministic normalization of Food and Agriculture telemetry into CCES
  while preserving food safety context, supply chain integrity, and evidentiary traceability.

- **State-Aware Correlation**  
  `CAI-USA-Food-and-Agriculture-Lab-State-Aware-Correlation.md`  
  Demonstrates correlation of CCES-normalized telemetry with explicit awareness of process
  state, food safety thresholds, and supply chain dependencies.

- **Telemetry Flow and Evidence**  
  `CAI-USA-Food-and-Agriculture-Lab-Telemetry-Flow-and-Evidence.md`  
  Demonstrates governed end-to-end telemetry flow and evidence generation, ensuring chain-of-
  custody, integrity, and audit readiness.

- **Human-Governed Decision Support**  
  `CAI-USA-Food-and-Agriculture-Lab-Human-Governed-Decision-Support.md`  
  Demonstrates strictly advisory, explainable decision support with mandatory human authority
  and explicit non-autonomy.
