## Purpose

The purpose of this directory is to define and contain the Information Technology sector overlay under the CAI USA framework.  
The Information Technology sector is treated as a foundational, first-class sector due to its transversal role across all other sectors, its responsibility for core digital infrastructure, and its central importance in identity, data integrity, availability, and trust preservation.

This sector establishes the baseline operational environment upon which other sector overlays depend and interoperate.

## Sector Overview

The Information Technology sector encompasses enterprise IT, cloud and hybrid infrastructure, identity and access management systems, endpoints, servers, networks, applications, platforms, and management planes that support organizational operations across all domains.

Information Technology environments act as the connective tissue between users, services, data, automation, and sector-specific systems. Compromise within this sector often enables cascading impact across multiple critical sectors simultaneously.

## Sector Characteristics

Information Technology environments are highly dynamic, continuously exposed, and identity-centric.  
They operate at scale, integrate with numerous third-party services, and serve as aggregation points for data, access, and control.

Threats in this sector commonly exploit identity, misconfiguration, trust relationships, automation pipelines, and management interfaces rather than purely technical vulnerabilities.

Operational priorities emphasize availability, integrity, trust continuity, and auditability while balancing speed of response with systemic stability.

## Relationship to CAI CORE

All Information Technology sector documents inherit CAI CORE lifecycle semantics, architecture, execution models, integrity governance, contamination prevention rules, and evolution controls without modification.

## Relationship to CAI USA Baseline

The Information Technology sector overlay extends the CAI USA baseline and refines it for IT-specific operational contexts.  
It operates consistently with CAI-USA-Definition, CAI-USA-CCES, CAI-USA-Telemetry-and-Data-Model, CAI-USA-Secure-by-Design-Controls, CAI-USA-AI-Governance-and-Assurance, CAI-USA-Threat-Model-Integration, and CAI-USA-Traceability-Matrix.

This sector does not redefine USA baseline semantics.
