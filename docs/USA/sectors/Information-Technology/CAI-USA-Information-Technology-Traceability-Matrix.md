This document defines auditable traceability relationships between CAI CORE, the CAI USA baseline, and the Information Technology sector overlay. It does not assert compliance, certification, accreditation, or regulatory approval.

Purpose

The purpose of this document is to establish explicit, auditable traceability between CAI CORE constructs, CAI USA baseline artifacts, and the Information Technology sector implementation.
This traceability matrix demonstrates inheritance, refinement, and constraint relationships without redefining authoritative framework elements.

This document exists to support architectural integrity, audit defensibility, and sector inheritance validation for Information Technology environments.

Scope

This traceability matrix applies exclusively to the CAI USA Information Technology sector.
It covers lifecycle semantics, operational execution, secure-by-design controls, AI governance, telemetry and data modeling, operations playbooks, and federally prioritized technical layers.

This document introduces no new requirements or interpretations. It records relationships only.

Traceability Relationship Types

Inherit
The Information Technology sector directly adopts the referenced element without modification.

Refine
The Information Technology sector adds contextual specificity while preserving original intent and authority.

Constrain
The Information Technology sector introduces stricter boundaries due to operational, governance, or trust constraints without redefining the underlying concept.
