## Purpose

The purpose of this directory is to define and contain the Transportation Systems sector overlay under the CAI USA framework. Transportation Systems are treated as a first-class, safety-critical sector due to their direct impact on human life, economic continuity, national mobility, and public trust. This sector overlay applies CAI in a manner that preserves safety dominance, operational continuity, accountability, and regulatory neutrality across diverse transportation domains.

## Sector Overview

The Transportation Systems sector includes organizations, systems, and environments that support the movement of people and goods across land, air, sea, rail, and pipeline infrastructures. This includes aviation-adjacent systems, rail networks, road and highway systems, maritime and port operations, mass transit, logistics platforms, traffic management systems, vehicle-to-infrastructure integrations, supporting IT, OT, CPS environments, and interconnected partner systems whose disruption could result in safety incidents, large-scale service outages, or cascading national impacts.

## Sector Characteristics

Transportation environments operate under strict safety, availability, and timing constraints. Systems are highly distributed, often legacy-heavy, and tightly coupled with physical processes and human operators. Downtime tolerance is low, recovery must be staged and predictable, and cybersecurity actions must never create unsafe operating conditions. Adversaries may target availability, integrity of control data, situational awareness, or public confidence rather than confidentiality alone.

## Relationship to CAI CORE

All Transportation Systems sector documents inherit CAI CORE lifecycle semantics, architecture, integrity governance, execution models, contamination prevention rules, and evolution controls. The seven-stage CAI lifecycle is applied without modification. CAI CORE remains authoritative for taxonomy, operational intent, integrity protection, and governance discipline.

## Relationship to CAI USA Baseline

This sector overlay extends the CAI USA baseline and refines it for Transportation Systems contexts. It operates alongside CAI-USA-Definition, CAI-USA-CCES, CAI-USA-Telemetry-and-Data-Model, CAI-USA-Secure-by-Design-Controls, CAI-USA-AI-Governance-and-Assurance, CAI-USA-ICS-OT-Safety-Gating, CAI-USA-Threat-Model-Integration, and CAI-USA-Traceability-Matrix. This sector does not redefine USA baseline semantics.
