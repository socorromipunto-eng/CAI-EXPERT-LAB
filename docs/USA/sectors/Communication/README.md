This README introduces the Communications sector under the CAI USA framework. It does not assert compliance, certification, accreditation, or regulatory approval.

Purpose
The purpose of this directory is to define and contain the Communications sector overlay under the CAI USA framework. Communications is treated as a first-class, safety- and continuity-critical sector due to its role as foundational infrastructure supporting national security, emergency services, economic activity, and the operation of all other critical infrastructure sectors. This sector overlay applies CAI in a manner that preserves availability, integrity, interoperability, and accountability across complex and highly interconnected communications environments.

Sector Overview
The Communications sector includes systems, networks, and services that enable the transmission of voice, data, video, signaling, and control information across wired, wireless, terrestrial, satellite, and hybrid infrastructures. This includes telecommunications backbones, carrier networks, internet exchange points, mobile and wireless systems, satellite communications, signaling and control platforms, network management systems, supporting IT and OT environments, and interconnection points whose disruption could cause cascading failures across dependent sectors.

Sector Characteristics
Communications environments are highly distributed, latency-sensitive, and interdependent with other critical infrastructure sectors. Availability and integrity are dominant priorities. Systems often operate at large scale, with heterogeneous technologies, vendor ecosystems, and long-lived infrastructure. Cyber incidents may propagate rapidly and have immediate national or cross-sector impact. Recovery actions must be coordinated, staged, and predictable. Human authority and operational coordination are mandatory for high-impact actions.

Relationship to CAI CORE
All Communications sector documents inherit CAI CORE lifecycle semantics, architecture, integrity governance, contamination prevention rules, and evolution controls. The seven-stage CAI lifecycle is applied without modification. CAI CORE remains authoritative for taxonomy, operational intent, integrity protection, and governance discipline.

Relationship to CAI USA Baseline
This sector overlay extends the CAI USA baseline and refines it for Communications contexts. It operates alongside CAI-USA-Definition, CAI-USA-CCES, CAI-USA-Telemetry-and-Data-Model, CAI-USA-Secure-by-Design-Controls, CAI-USA-AI-Governance-and-Assurance, CAI-USA-ICS-OT-Safety-Gating where applicable, CAI-USA-Threat-Model-Integration, and CAI-USA-Traceability-Matrix. This sector does not redefine USA baseline semantics.
