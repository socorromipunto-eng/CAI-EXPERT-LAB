## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to formally declare the CAI USA Aviation sector overlay stable and frozen at version 0.1 (Draft).  
This freeze establishes Aviation as a reference sector implementation under the CAI USA framework.

## Scope

This freeze applies to all documents contained under the directory docs/USA/Aviation/.  
It covers sector definition, threat and assumption modeling, safety gating and operational constraints, secure-by-design controls, AI governance and assurance, telemetry and data modeling, operations playbooks, and traceability artifacts.

## Freeze Conditions

As of this declaration, the CAI USA Aviation sector overlay is considered architecturally complete and stable.  
No document within the Aviation sector overlay may be modified without an explicit sector version increment and a corresponding entry in the CAI-Core-Evolution-Traceability-Register.

Any future modification must preserve CAI CORE semantics, CAI USA baseline boundaries, interoperability principles, and the explicit non-claim posture of the framework.

## Relationship to CAI CORE and CAI USA Baseline

This freeze does not alter CAI CORE or CAI USA baseline documents.  
The Aviation sector overlay remains a consumer of CORE and USA baseline semantics and does not redefine or override them.
