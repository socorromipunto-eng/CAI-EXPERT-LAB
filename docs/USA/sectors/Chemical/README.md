## Purpose

The purpose of this directory is to define and contain the Chemical sector overlay for the Cybersecurity Artificial Intelligence Framework under the CAI USA baseline.

The Chemical sector is treated as a first-class sector due to its safety-critical processes, hazardous material handling, complex supply chains, environmental risk exposure, and potential for large-scale public health and national security impact resulting from cyber incidents.

This sector overlay establishes how CAI is applied coherently within Chemical environments while preserving safety, containment, accountability, and regulatory neutrality.

## Sector Characteristics

Chemical environments commonly involve continuous or batch processes, hazardous materials, precise control parameters, and tightly coupled dependencies between IT systems, OT systems, physical processes, and human operators.

Cyber incidents in this sector may result in uncontrolled reactions, toxic releases, environmental contamination, worker safety incidents, and cascading impacts across Emergency Services, Healthcare, Transportation, and Energy sectors.

Operational predictability, integrity, and containment are dominant priorities.

## Sector Governance Intent

The Chemical sector overlay refines CAI execution to enforce conservative operational behavior, strict safety gating, deterministic execution, and mandatory human authority for any action that may affect physical processes or hazardous material handling.

Automation and AI usage are explicitly bounded and governed to prevent unsafe states, uncontrolled reactions, or loss of accountability.

## Directory Contents

This directory contains sector-specific documents that extend the CAI USA baseline without redefining CAI CORE semantics.

Expected documents include Sector Definition, Threat and Assumption Model, Secure-by-Design Controls, AI Governance and Assurance, Telemetry and Data Model, Operations Playbooks, and Traceability Matrix.

Each document inherits CAI CORE and CAI USA baseline principles and refines them for Chemical sector constraints.

## Relationship to CAI CORE

All documents in this directory inherit lifecycle semantics, governance rules, integrity protections, contamination prevention requirements, and evolution controls defined in CAI CORE.

CAI CORE remains authoritative.

## Relationship to CAI USA Baseline

This sector overlay extends the CAI USA baseline and remains consistent with CAI-USA-Definition, CAI-USA-CCES, CAI-USA-Telemetry-and-Data-Model, CAI-USA-Secure-by-Design-Controls, CAI-USA-AI-Governance-and-Assurance, CAI-USA-ICS-OT-Safety-Gating, CAI-USA-Threat-Model-Integration, and CAI-USA-Traceability-Matrix.
