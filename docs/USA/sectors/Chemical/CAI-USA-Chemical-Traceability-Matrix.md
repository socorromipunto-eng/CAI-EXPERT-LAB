This document defines the traceability relationships between CAI CORE, the CAI USA baseline, and the CAI USA Chemical sector. It does not assert compliance, certification, accreditation, or regulatory approval.

Purpose

The purpose of this document is to provide explicit, auditable traceability between CAI CORE constructs, CAI USA baseline artifacts, and the Chemical sector implementation.

This matrix demonstrates inheritance, refinement, and constraint relationships without redefining or duplicating authoritative framework elements. It exists to support architectural integrity, audit defensibility, and sector inheritance validation.

Scope

This traceability matrix applies exclusively to the CAI USA Chemical sector.

It covers lifecycle semantics, secure-by-design controls, AI governance, telemetry and data modeling, operational playbooks, safety dominance constraints, and federally prioritized technical layers.

This document records relationships only and introduces no new requirements or interpretations.

Traceability Model

The traceability model used in this document follows three relationship types.

Inherit
The Chemical sector directly adopts the referenced element without modification.

Refine
The Chemical sector adds contextual specificity while preserving original intent and authority.

Constrain
The Chemical sector introduces stricter boundaries due to safety, environmental, or operational constraints without redefining the underlying concept.
