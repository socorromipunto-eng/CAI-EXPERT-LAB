This document defines traceability relationships between CAI CORE, the CAI USA baseline, and the CAI USA Defense Industrial Base sector. It does not assert compliance, certification, accreditation, or regulatory approval.

Purpose
The purpose of this document is to provide explicit, auditable traceability between CAI CORE constructs, CAI USA baseline artifacts, and the Defense Industrial Base sector overlay. This matrix demonstrates inheritance, refinement, and constraint relationships required to preserve architectural integrity, national-security relevance, governance discipline, and audit defensibility without redefining authoritative framework elements.

Scope
This traceability matrix applies exclusively to the CAI USA Defense Industrial Base sector. It covers lifecycle semantics, governance, secure-by-design controls, AI governance, telemetry, operations playbooks, safety constraints, and federally prioritized technical layers. This document records relationships only and introduces no new requirements or interpretations.

Traceability Model
The traceability model uses three relationship types.

Inherit
The Defense Industrial Base sector adopts the referenced element without modification.

Refine
The Defense Industrial Base sector adds contextual specificity while preserving original authority and intent.

Constrain
The Defense Industrial Base sector applies stricter limits due to national security, contractual, intelligence, or operational risk without redefining the underlying concept.
