## Purpose

The purpose of this directory is to define and contain the Defense Industrial Base sector overlay under the CAI USA framework. The DIB sector is treated as a first-class, high-criticality sector due to its direct relationship with national defense, controlled information, sensitive supply chains, and its exposure to advanced, persistent, and well-resourced adversaries. This sector overlay applies CAI in a manner that preserves national security relevance, operational accountability, and strict governance boundaries.

## Sector Overview

The Defense Industrial Base sector includes organizations, systems, and environments that design, manufacture, maintain, integrate, or support defense-related capabilities, technologies, and services. This includes prime contractors, subcontractors, suppliers, research and development entities, and supporting IT, OT, and hybrid environments whose compromise could impact national security, military readiness, or defense trust relationships.

## Sector Characteristics

DIB environments operate under heightened confidentiality, integrity, and availability expectations. Assets are long-lived, supply chains are complex and globally distributed, and trust relationships are tightly controlled. Adversaries are assumed to be patient, well-funded, and capable of multi-stage operations, including supply-chain compromise, insider exploitation, and long-term persistence. Cybersecurity operations must preserve evidence, attribution potential, and legal accountability.

## Relationship to CAI CORE

All Defense Industrial Base sector documents inherit CAI CORE lifecycle semantics, architecture, integrity governance, execution models, and evolution controls. The CAI seven-stage lifecycle is applied without modification. CAI CORE remains authoritative for taxonomy, operational intent, integrity protection, and contamination prevention.

## Relationship to CAI USA Baseline

This sector overlay extends the CAI USA baseline and refines it for Defense Industrial Base contexts. It operates alongside CAI-USA-Definition, CAI-USA-CCES, CAI-USA-Telemetry-and-Data-Model, CAI-USA-Secure-by-Design-Controls, CAI-USA-AI-Governance-and-Assurance, CAI-USA-ICS-OT-Safety-Gating, CAI-USA-Threat-Model-Integration, and CAI-USA-Traceability-Matrix. This sector does not redefine USA baseline semantics.
