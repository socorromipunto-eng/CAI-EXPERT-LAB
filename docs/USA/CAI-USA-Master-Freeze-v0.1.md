Purpose

The purpose of this document is to formally declare the CAI USA framework frozen at version 0.1.
This master freeze establishes a controlled, stable, and auditable baseline for the CAI USA implementation, including the USA baseline documents and all defined USA sector overlays.

This freeze is intended to preserve architectural integrity, prevent semantic drift, enforce change discipline, and enable external technical review without destabilizing the framework.

Scope

This master freeze applies to all CAI USA artifacts produced under the CAI-EXPERT-LAB project as of version 0.1.
It includes the CAI USA baseline documents and all USA sector overlays defined in the original CAI sector design.

This document does not freeze CAI CORE. CAI CORE remains authoritative and governed by its own evolution and integrity controls.

Freeze Declaration

Effective upon publication of this document, the CAI USA baseline and all listed sector overlays are frozen at version 0.1.

No document within the frozen scope may be modified, restructured, or semantically altered without initiating a formal version increment and recording the change in the CAI-Core-Evolution-Traceability-Register.

Formatting corrections that do not alter meaning or semantics are permitted but must not introduce structural or conceptual changes.

Post-Freeze Change Control

Any proposed modification to a frozen CAI USA artifact must satisfy all of the following conditions.

Explicit justification for change

Assessment of impact on CAI CORE inheritance

Preservation of non-claim posture

Traceability to a documented governance decision

Version increment beyond v0.1

Uncontrolled edits, ad hoc refinements, or AI-generated modifications without governance review are prohibited.

Relationship to CAI CORE

This master freeze does not alter, restrict, or redefine CAI CORE.
CAI CORE remains authoritative for lifecycle semantics, execution models, integrity governance, contamination prevention rules, and standards evolution.

CAI USA continues to inherit CAI CORE without redefining or overriding core concepts.

Relationship to Future Regional Frameworks

This master freeze establishes CAI USA v0.1 as the stable reference baseline for future regional frameworks, including but not limited to CAI EU.

Future regional implementations must inherit from CAI CORE and may reference CAI USA as a comparative or foundational implementation but must not modify frozen USA artifacts.

Explicit Non-Claims

This document does not assert compliance with NIST, CISA, CMMC, DoD, DHS, or any other regulatory or standards authority.
It does not certify systems, authorize operational deployment, or replace regulatory governance.

It declares architectural state and governance control only.

Status

Frozen – CAI USA framework v0.1 established.
