## 1. Purpose of This Document

This document formally declares the Master Freeze of the CAI EU regional
baseline, version 0.1.

Its purpose is to:

- Establish a stable, auditable reference point

- Prevent uncontrolled drift of governance semantics

- Enable external review, academic analysis, and internal assurance

- Clearly separate future evolution from the frozen baseline

This freeze applies only to CAI EU v0.1 and does not affect CAI Core or
other regional baselines.

## 2. Scope of the Freeze

The following CAI EU artifacts are included in this freeze:

- Foundational Governance

- CAI-EU-Foundational-Governance.md

- CAI-EU-Risk-and-Proportionality-Model.md

- CAI-EU-Interoperability-and-Sector-Neutrality.md

- Sector Overlays

- CAI-EU-Public-Sector-Governance-Overlay.md

- CAI-EU-Critical-Infrastructure-Governance-Overlay.md (planned; not yet written and not covered by this freeze)

- CAI-EU-Research-and-Academia-Governance-Overlay.md

- Transversal Assurance Artifacts

- CAI-EU-Governance-Traceability-Matrix.md

- CAI-EU-Sector-Overlay-Index-and-Navigation.md

- CAI-EU-Cross-Sector-Risk-Coordination-Note.md

Together, these artifacts constitute the complete CAI EU v0.1 baseline.

## 3. Freeze Semantics

From the date of this declaration:

- No modifications are permitted to frozen artifacts

- No semantic reinterpretation is allowed

- No retroactive alignment or optimization is authorized

- No sector-specific extensions may alter frozen content

Corrections are limited to non-semantic fixes (e.g., typographical errors),
and must be explicitly documented as such.

## 4. Relationship to CAI Core and Other Regions

This freeze:

- Does not modify CAI Core

- Does not imply CAI Core freeze

- Does not affect CAI USA or any other regional baseline

CAI Core remains authoritative and unchanged.

## 5. Forward Evolution Policy

Future evolution of CAI EU must occur through:

- New versions (e.g., CAI EU v0.2, v1.0)

- Explicit change documentation

- Clear differentiation between frozen and evolving artifacts

No future work may retroactively modify CAI EU v0.1 semantics.

## 6. Governance Rationale

Freezing CAI EU v0.1 reflects:

- Maturity of foundational governance

- Completion of initial sector overlays

- Demonstrated interoperability and traceability

- Readiness for review without compliance claims

This freeze does not assert completeness, only governance stability.

## 7. Non-Scope Clarifications

This freeze does not:

- Assert regulatory compliance

- Imply endorsement by any authority

- Prevent future research or evolution

- Restrict experimental or derivative work outside v0.1

It solely preserves the integrity of the v0.1 baseline.
