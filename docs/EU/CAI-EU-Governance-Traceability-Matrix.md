1. Purpose of This Document

This document establishes a governance traceability matrix for the
CAI EU regional baseline.

Its purpose is to provide structured visibility into how CAI EU
governance principles:

Derive from CAI Core

Are consistently applied across EU foundational documents

Are preserved across sector-specific overlays

This matrix supports auditability, academic review, and governance
assurance, without asserting compliance or regulatory alignment.

2. Nature of Governance Traceability

Governance traceability within CAI EU:

Is conceptual and structural, not regulatory

Focuses on inheritance and constraint, not mapping

Demonstrates consistency and non-contradiction

This matrix does not map CAI EU to legal articles, regulatory clauses,
or certification criteria.

3. Traceability Dimensions

The matrix traces governance across the following dimensions:

Authority hierarchy (CAI Core → CAI EU → Sector Overlays)

Human authority and non-autonomy of AI

Risk- and proportionality-based constraint

Evidence, traceability, and reviewability

Sector neutrality and interoperability

Each dimension is examined across documents, not across regulations.

4. Core-to-EU Traceability
Governance Principle	CAI Core	CAI EU Foundational Docs	EU Sector Overlays
Human authority	Defined	Reinforced	Preserved
AI non-autonomy	Explicit	Explicit	Explicit
Accountability	Core requirement	Contextualized	Sector-applied
Evidence integrity	Required	Scaled by risk	Sector-aware
Governance precedence	Absolute	Enforced	Inherited

This table illustrates inheritance without reinterpretation.

5. EU Foundational Traceability
Principle	Governance	Risk & Proportionality	Interoperability
Risk-governed AI use	Defined	Operationalized	Preserved
Proportional oversight	Declared	Structured	Maintained
Sector neutrality	Required	Respected	Enforced
Human decision primacy	Absolute	Scaled	Uniform

Foundational documents are mutually reinforcing, not overlapping.

6. Sector Overlay Traceability
Overlay	Additional Constraints	Preserved Principles
Public Sector	Democratic accountability, transparency	Human authority, non-autonomy
Critical Infrastructure	Systemic risk, cascading impact awareness	Proportionality, evidence integrity
Research & Academia	Experimental accountability, reproducibility	Traceability, human oversight

No overlay introduces new authority, only additional constraints.

7. Interoperability Preservation

Traceability confirms that:

All overlays inherit the same governance baseline

No sector creates governance exceptions

Cross-sector reasoning remains possible

Evidence and accountability remain comparable

This prevents fragmentation across European sectors.

8. Audit and Review Utility

This matrix supports:

Internal governance reviews

External audit preparation

Academic analysis and critique

Policy and standards dialogue

It does not serve as a compliance checklist or certification artifact.

9. Non-Scope Clarifications

This document does not:

Provide an article-level mapping to EU regulations or directives (planned; alignment is declared in CAI-EU-Foundational-Governance section 8 and REGULATORY-BASIS.md)

Define compliance controls

Establish conformity criteria

Replace legal interpretation

Its role is governance coherence, not regulatory mapping.

10. Forward Constraint

All future CAI EU documents must be traceable through this matrix.

Untraceable artifacts indicate:

Governance drift

Loss of interoperability

Increased audit risk

Such artifacts require review or rejection.
