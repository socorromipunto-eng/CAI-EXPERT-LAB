## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the intended architectural, semantic, and
operational scope and completeness criteria of **CAI Core v0.1**, the foundational layer
of the Cybersecurity Artificial Intelligence Framework (CAI). Its completeness remains
under review as a research preview; this document does not establish that all intended
capabilities have been implemented or validated.

This declaration exists to eliminate ambiguity for reviewers, auditors, implementers, and
governing bodies regarding what CAI Core **does**, **does not**, and **is not intended to**
cover.

---

## Definition of CAI Core Completeness

CAI Core v0.1 is considered complete when it provides, in a stable and governed manner:

- A universal architectural definition independent of jurisdiction or regulation
- A deterministic operational state model
- An authoritative execution model
- A controlled governance and integrity framework
- Explicit rules preventing contamination, ambiguity, or silent behavior changes
- Full auditability and traceability of decisions, states, and evolution
- A normative taxonomy and vocabulary
- Explicit non-autonomy and human-oversight principles

All of the above elements are present and defined in the CAI Core documentation set.

---

## Scope of CAI Core

CAI Core defines **foundational semantics and governance**, including:

- Conceptual and logical architecture of CAI
- Operational state lifecycle and transitions
- Execution semantics and authority boundaries
- Governance, integrity, and self-defense mechanisms
- Audit, traceability, and evolution control
- Taxonomy and normative vocabulary
- Threat modeling for framework integrity

CAI Core is intentionally **non-regional**, **non-regulatory**, and **non-sector-specific**.

---

## Explicit Non-Scope of CAI Core

CAI Core explicitly does **not** define or include:

- Jurisdictional or regulatory compliance requirements
- Regional baselines (e.g., USA, EU, LATAM)
- Sector-specific operational profiles
- Implementation-specific tooling or deployment instructions
- Certification, accreditation, or compliance claims
- Performance guarantees or optimization strategies
- Autonomous execution authority

These elements are handled by regional baselines, sector overlays, laboratories, or future
extensions.

---

## Relationship to CAI Engine

CAI Core defines the **authoritative semantics** and governance rules that the CAI Engine
implements.

The CAI Engine:
- Must conform to CAI Core definitions
- May not redefine Core semantics
- May not bypass governance, integrity, or safety constraints
- Operates as an execution-layer realization of Core intent

CAI Core does not depend on any specific CAI Engine implementation.

---

## Relationship to Regional Baselines (e.g., CAI USA)

Regional baselines, such as **CAI USA**, extend CAI Core by introducing jurisdictional,
operational, and sector-aligned constraints without modifying CAI Core semantics.

CAI Core:
- Is authoritative over all regional baselines
- Remains unchanged by regional adaptations
- Provides the stable foundation upon which regional frameworks are built

---

## Relationship to Extensions

Optional extensions may build upon CAI Core capabilities but must not redefine, override, or
bypass Core semantics.

Extensions are:
- Optional by design
- Non-authoritative
- Independently versioned
- Governed under explicit constraints

The existence or absence of extensions does not affect CAI Core completeness.

---

## Versioning and Evolution

CAI Core v0.1 is published as a research preview for reference implementation, analysis,
and controlled adoption. Its completeness is under review.

Future versions of CAI Core:
- Will be introduced only through explicit governance processes
- Will preserve backward semantic integrity unless explicitly declared otherwise
- Will be documented through formal evolution and traceability mechanisms

---

## Applicability Statement

CAI Core v0.1 is suitable for:
- Framework analysis and technical review
- Reference architecture development
- Regional baseline construction
- Sector overlay design
- Academic and applied research

CAI Core v0.1 is **not** a certification standard, compliance framework, or regulatory mandate.
